Virtual Acoustic Object (VAO) 0.6.0

VAO describes digital resources and packages their files for exchange. Its 0.6.0 mapping uses the MODAVIS Ontology Network 0.1.0.

The VAO mapping relates logical assets to MODAVIS digital assets and realizations to MODAVIS bitstreams. A VAO manifest can also use MODAVIS instrument and organ terms directly. The dependency runs from VAO to MODAVIS.

MMIC 1.0.0 is VAO’s optional profile for mechanical music carriers such as rolls, discs, and barrels. Its ontology and validation files are part of the VAO release.
